# Theki Studios — Final Design Direction

Theki Studios is a restrained editorial portfolio for a photography practice. The site is built around one idea: let the work lead. Large, high-contrast photographs sit in a quiet interface with warm paper tones, dark ink text, and a muted brass accent. The overall aesthetic is calm, premium, and almost gallery-like rather than overly digital.

## Visual language

- Warm neutral palette: paper, clay, ink, and brass
- Condensed display typography for titles and chapter headings
- Minimal framing and lots of whitespace, with content breathing around the images
- A slow, deliberate motion system: reveal, settle, dim, and drift rather than flashy transitions
- Strong image hierarchy: the photograph is always the primary object, never decorative filler

## Structure

The page opens with a confident hero that frames the studio and the work as a visual practice. Below that, a chapter-based portfolio section moves through the major collections in a scroll-led narrative. Each chapter is treated like a gallery room: the title arrives, the images settle into place, and the visitor reads the story through motion and pacing rather than gimmicks.

The site also includes an editorial service index, concise contact section, and footer that keeps the experience polished without becoming cluttered. Navigation is intentionally sparse and functional, matching the broader design ethos.

## Motion and interaction

Motion is subtle and tied to scroll. Elements appear with soft vertical reveal, photos settle into place with gentle scaling, and prior images dim as the next one comes forward. This creates a clear editorial rhythm without distracting from the image itself. There are no noisy overlays, cinematic HUD elements, or artificial “effects.” Reduced-motion support remains in place so the experience stays accessible and comfortable.

## Content approach

The copy is direct and understated. It supports the imagery but never competes with it. Services are presented as a clean index, and all portfolio chapters share the same editorial treatment so the experience feels cohesive from start to finish.

## Technical intent

The implementation uses Vite, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS with a lean motion stack built around scroll-driven behavior. The code is structured to keep styling and animation values easy to refine while preserving the calm visual character. The goal is a fast, accessible, high-quality portfolio that feels timeless and quietly luxurious.

## Outcome

The final design is not a tech demo. It is a modern photography website that feels like a quiet exhibition space: tactile, atmospheric, and intentionally simple. The visitor should understand the studio immediately, feel the quality of the work, and move through the portfolio without friction.
